2. Page Functionality by Role (View vs. Create / Edit / Delete)

Exact breakdown of what each role can do on each screen, highlighting restricted vs open capabilities.

Role Authority Breakdown
📅

Spotlight: Company Leave Calendar (/calendar/company-leave)

Public holidays, state holidays, and gazetted company shutdowns

CRUD Restricted Strictly to HR
👑 HR Admin Role (Full CRUD) Create + Edit + Delete
  • "+ Add Company Leave" Button: Visible in top PageActions bar.
  • Creation Dialog: Can set Holiday Name, Start Date, End Date, Applies To (All Staff, Branch, Department), Paid Status, and Attendance Required.
  • Action Column Enabled: Displays Edit (pencil) and Delete (trash) buttons on every active holiday row.
  • Audit Protection: Even for HR Admin, once a leave reaches Completed status, Edit and Delete are permanently disabled.
👥 All Other Roles (View Only) View Only

Applies to Employee, Branch Officer, Branch Leader, Head of Department, Operation Manager, Finance Manager, Managing Director:

  • Can View: All upcoming gazetted holidays, dates, and branch applicability.
  • "+ Add Company Leave" Button: COMPLETELY HIDDEN from the DOM.
  • "Actions" Column: COMPLETELY OMITTED from the table.
  • No Edit or Delete: Other users cannot create, modify, or remove company off days.

🗓️ Work Calendar (/calendar)

Employee / Officer: View personal working schedule, personal approved leaves, and assigned outstation duties.

Branch Leader / HOD: View personal roster + overlay of subordinates' approved leaves and shifts in their branch/department.

HR Admin / Management: Global multi-branch work calendar view.

⏱️ Team Attendance (/team-attendance)

Employee / Officer: Access Blocked (hidden from sidebar).

Branch Leader: Live view of clock-in status (Present, Late, Absent) strictly for staff at their physical branch.

Head of Department: Live view of clock-in status strictly for personnel in their department across all locations.

HR Admin / MD: Global presence board with branch/department filters.

📋 Team Leave Requests (/leave/team)

Employee: Access Blocked (uses /leave/forms to track own requests).

Branch Leader (Tier 1): Approve or Reject leave for branch staff; inspect uploaded Medical Certificate (MC) document.

Head of Department (Tier 2): Endorse or Reject departmental leave applications.

HR Admin / Management: Central administrative review.

🏛️ Leave Admin Hub (/leave/admin)

Employee / Officer: Access Blocked.

Branch Leader / HOD: View pending items awaiting their tier.

HR Admin / MD / Operation Manager: Grant final endorsements, perform batch approvals, and export leave records to CSV / PDF.

📊 Leave Entitlements (/leave/entitlement)

HR Admin: Exclusive Authority. Can manually adjust (+/- days) Annual, Sick, Emergency, or Replacement Leave with compulsory audit remarks.

All Other Roles: Hidden / No access.

👥 Employee Directory (/employees)

Employee: View-only contact lookup (colleague names, email, branch). "+ Add Staff" is hidden.

Branch Leader / HOD: View branch/dept staff profiles and phone numbers. Cannot create or edit.

HR Admin / MD / Operation Manager: "+ Add Staff" modal, edit profiles, deactivate resigned staff.

4. Leave Application & Multi-Tier Approval Pipeline

Comprehensive management across 5 leave types with automated multi-tier approval.

3-Tier Approval Flow
Type 1
Annual Leave (AL)

Paid vacation deducted from annual quota (default 14 days).

Type 2
Medical / Sick (MC)

Paid sick leave. Mandatory clinic Medical Certificate slip upload.

Type 3
Emergency Leave (EL)

Urgent sudden leave (default 5 days). Direct supervisor notification.

Type 4
Replacement (Cuti Ganti)

Earned compensatory leave from public holidays or approved OT.

Type 5
Unpaid (Tanpa Gaji)

Unpaid absence leading to automatic payroll salary deduction.

Multi-Tier Approval Hierarchy

Tier 1: Branch Level Branch Leader

Reviews and endorses leave for branch officers and local retail staff.

Tier 2: Department Level Head of Dept (HOD)

Reviews staffing impact on departmental operations across all branches.

Tier 3: Executive Level HR Admin / MD / OM

Final endorsement, payroll deduction approval, and batch processing.

5. Attendance Configuration & Late Grace Window

Where to set late thresholds, punch grace periods, and geofence enforcement.

Settings: Tab 1
⚙️

Location: Settings Hub → Tab 1: Attendance Config

Route: /settings?tab=attendance (HR Admin & Managing Director Only)

1. Late Threshold (Grace Window)

Configurable grace window in minutes (e.g. 30 minutes). Punches within the grace window flag as Late but do not trigger harsh half-day cuts.

2. Early Checkout Tolerance

Defines allowed minutes before official shift end (e.g. 0 to 15 minutes) before flagging early departure penalties.

3. Geofence Modes

Strict: Blocks punch button outside radius.
Relaxed: Allows punch but flags violation.
Disabled: Remote mode.

⚡ Built-in Calculation Simulator:

At the bottom of this tab, administrators can input hypothetical clock-in times (e.g. 08:45 AM) against shift rosters to simulate and verify whether late tags or overtime rules trigger accurately.

6. Work Schedules: Company & Branch Configuration

Configure shifts, Malaysian weekend zones (Zone A vs Zone B), and branch assignments.

Settings: Tab 6
📅

Location: Settings Hub → Tab 6: Work Schedules

Route: /settings?tab=schedule (HR Admin, MD, Operation Manager)

Zone A (Friday-Saturday Weekend) East Coast / North

Applies to branches in Kelantan, Terengganu, Kedah, and Johor. Working days are Sunday through Thursday. Friday and Saturday are designated rest days.

Zone B (Saturday-Sunday Weekend) West Coast / Federal

Applies to branches in Kuala Lumpur, Selangor, Perak, Penang, Melaka, Pahang, Sabah, Sarawak. Working days are Monday through Friday.

Multi-Branch Scoping: A single schedule can be mapped to multiple selected branches at once (e.g. mapping Zone A schedule simultaneously to Kemaman, KT, Dungun, and Jertih).

Special Shift Rules: Supports Friday extended prayer breaks (e.g. 12:30 - 14:30) and half-day working hours on Thursdays or Saturdays.

7. New Subsidiary Setup Lifecycle (From Zero to Fully Operational Data)

The mandatory sequential setup pipeline required before a newly created company can operate and display full data.

Sequential Requirement Chain

💡 Why a New Subsidiary Starts Blank and Requires Sequential Setup

When HR creates a new subsidiary entity (e.g. RayRent Car Rental Sdn Bhd or Rayhar Holidays), it initially possesses zero branches, zero work schedules, unconfigured leave policies, and zero employees. If staff were assigned immediately without branches or shifts, clock-ins would fail (no GPS coordinates) and late penalties could not be computed (no working hours). Following the 7-Step Sequential Pipeline below guarantees that all operational dependencies match, fully activating the subsidiary across all dashboards and reports.

1

Step 1: Register Corporate Entity & Subsidiary

Route: /settings?tab=company (Settings Hub: 3. COMPANY MANAGEMENT)

Entity Provisioning

Click "+ Add Company". Enter Company Legal Name (e.g. RayRent Sdn Bhd), Company Type (Subsidiary), Parent Company (Rayhar Travels), SSM number, address, and upload brand logo via CompanyLogoUploader. Set Status to Active and save.

2

Step 2: Create Branches with Map GPS Coordinates & Geofencing

Route: /settings?tab=branch (Settings Hub: 4. BRANCH & LOCATION)

Geofence Anchoring

Select the new company from the dropdown. Click "+ Add Branch". Enter Branch Code (e.g. RR-SEPANG), Branch Name (e.g. RayRent Airport Hub). Use the interactive MapLibre search & pin placement to drop the exact GPS coordinates. Set the Geofence Radius Slider (e.g. 100 meters).

3

Step 3: Configure Working Hours & Shift Schedules for that Branch

Route: /settings?tab=schedule (Settings Hub: 6. WORK SCHEDULES)

Shift Scheduling

Select the new company entity. Click "+ Create Work Schedule". Select Zone A (Fri-Sat weekend) or Zone B (Sat-Sun weekend). Select the branches created in Step 2. Configure 7-day shift hours (e.g. 08:30 to 17:30), working days, and lunch breaks (13:00 - 14:00).

4

Step 4: Configure Leave Types & Specific Quotas (e.g. AL = 4 Days)

Route: /settings?tab=leave (Settings Hub: 7. LEAVE & APPROVALS)

Entitlement Rules

Select the new company. Customize the default leave entitlements for this specific entity: Set Annual Leave (AL) directly in the number box (e.g. set to 4 Days for subsidiary terms instead of standard 14). Configure Sick Leave (14 days), Emergency Leave (5 days), Unpaid Leave max cap, and enable mandatory Medical Certificate (MC) slip upload.

5

Step 5: Establish Multi-Tier Leave Approval Routing Rules

Route: /settings?tab=leave (ApprovalBuilder Section)

Workflow Pipelines

Scroll down to the ApprovalBuilder for the new subsidiary. Click "+ Add Workflow". Assign which branches trigger this pipeline and define the multi-tier escalation ladder: Tier 1 (Branch Leader) $\rightarrow$ Tier 2 (HOD) $\rightarrow$ Tier 3 (Operation Manager / HR Admin).

6

Step 6: Provision / Transfer Staff Members to the Subsidiary

Routes: /settings?tab=employee or /organization/transfer

Staff Population

Now assign human personnel: Register new hires via the Onboarding Form by choosing the new Company Entity and its branch/department, or migrate existing experienced staff from the parent company using the Company Transfer Wizard.

7

Step 7: FULLY ACTIVATED — How to View & Access All Data for that Company

All Requirements Matched: Complete Data Visibility Unlocked

Operational & Live

Once Steps 1 through 6 are fulfilled, HR and Management can inspect the subsidiary's complete operational telemetry:

1. Global Company Switcher: Click the Company Switcher at the top of the header bar. Select the subsidiary (e.g. RayRent Sdn Bhd). The entire portal context instantly switches.
2. Live Dashboard & Attendance: Dashboard (/) displays real-time attendance, tardiness, and absences exclusively for the subsidiary's staff based on its custom branch coordinates and shift hours.
3. Leave & Approvals Roster: /leave/admin displays leaves routed through the configured 3-tier sequence, correctly checking against the configured AL quota (e.g. 4 days).
4. Payroll Reports & Analytics: /reports and /hr-analytics exports monthly attendance logs, overtime, and unpaid deductions filtered strictly for the subsidiary's payroll.

8. Temporary Branch Deployments & Entity Transfers

Deploying staff to other branches and cross-company entity migration.

Dynamic Geofence Re-targeting

📍 Temporary Branch Deployment (/branches/temporary-assignments)

When an employee is temporarily sent to another branch (e.g. HQ officer sent to Kemaman for 2 weeks):

  • HR Admin / Branch Leader specifies the destination branch and date range.
  • Automatic Geofence Shift: During that window, the employee clocks in against Kemaman coordinates instead of HQ.
  • Once the end date arrives, geofencing seamlessly reverts to their home branch.

🔄 Company Transfer Wizard (/organization/transfer)

For permanently transferring an employee between subsidiaries (e.g. from Rayhar Travels to RayRent):

  • Select Target Entity, new Branch, and new Role.
  • Set Effective Transfer Date.
  • Appends an immutable log to the Employee Company History Ledger (/organization/history).
